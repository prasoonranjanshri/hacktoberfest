# Features and Typologies

Timestamps are naive local-time ISO strings. Amounts are INR.
Design Decision (Ablation Boundary): "Behaviour-only" features are restricted strictly to properties computable from an account's own outgoing transactions and profile alone, WITHOUT any flow relationship between incoming and outgoing money. Inflows (txn_count_in, total_in_inr) are network/flow properties that, when combined with outflow totals, allow decision trees to reconstruct flow balance and forwarding ratios. Therefore, txn_count_in and total_in_inr belong to network features, ensuring the behaviour-only baseline isolates pure account behaviour from network flow dynamics.

Behaviour features (per account): txn_count_out; total_out_inr; avg_amount_out (0 if none); night_txn_ratio = share of outgoing txns between 00:00 and 05:00 (0 if none); new_payee_ratio = share of outgoing txns that are the first txn from this account to that receiver (0 if none); max_out_txns_10min = max number of outgoing txns in any 10-minute window; account_age_days = days since opening at the end of the simulation.
Network features: txn_count_in; total_in_inr; n_unique_senders; n_unique_receivers; forward_ratio_15min = (sum of outgoing amounts that occur within 15 minutes after any incoming payment, capped at total_in_inr) / total_in_inr, 0 if no incoming, clipped to [0,1]; median_hold_minutes = median over incoming txns of the minutes until the account's next outgoing txn, capped at 1440, and 1440 if none or no incoming; neighbour_mean_forward_ratio = mean forward_ratio_15min over distinct counterparties (senders and receivers), 0 if none.
Simulator defaults: 3,000 accounts, 30 days, seed 42. 150 fraud accounts (5%, chosen for demo statistical power; real-world rates are far lower and the pitch must say so).
- fan_in_fan_out: 12 rings x (1 collector + 4 mules) = 60 accounts. Each mule receives 6-15 payments from distinct ordinary accounts within a few hours (INR 2,000-25,000), forwards 85-95% to the ring collector within 2-15 minutes. Noise: 20% of mules forward slowly (30-120 min), 20% forward only 50-70%. Mules are mostly new accounts (5-60 days); 25% have older ages (200-800 days).
- pass_through_chain: 8 chains x 6 accounts = 48. The entry account receives from 2-4 senders and passes the money hop by hop within 3-20 minutes, keeping about 5% per hop. Noise: 20% of hops are slower.
- burst_velocity: 42 compromised accounts with ordinary history, then 15-40 outgoing payments within 10 minutes between 00:00 and 05:00 to 8-20 receivers, INR 500-5,000.
Normal accounts (2,850): about 87% ordinary (1-15 outgoing txns per month to 1-6 regular receivers, lognormal amounts median about INR 800, daytime); about 5% merchants (20-80 distinct senders, forward less than 10%, hard negative for fan-in); about 2% payroll accounts (a few incoming batches, payments to 10-40 receivers, hold hours); about 1.5% aggregators (receive and forward within minutes to many distinct receivers in daytime, hard negative for forwarding speed); about 4.5% night-active ordinary accounts (hard negative for night ratio). Account ages 30-2000 days.
